    What it does

    Breathe in and out with the spiral. Each breath is shorter than the last, 13 seconds down to 1, until there's nothing left to count.

    The cadence is the Fibonacci sequence, run backwards: 13, 8, 5, 3, 2, 1. In the Fibonacci sequence, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, each number is the sum of the two before it. You breathe in for 13 seconds and out for 13, then in for 8 and out for 8, and so on down to 1.

    The six numbers add up to 32, and every number is used twice, once in and once out. So the whole thing takes exactly 64 seconds, which is about one minute. The softer start skips the 13 and takes 38 seconds.

    Every breath is both an inhale and an exhale of the same length. The bars shrink toward stillness.

    Why Fibonacci

    Divide any Fibonacci number by the one before it and you get closer and closer to 1.618…, the golden ratio, φ. So each breath here is roughly 0.618 of the one before it.

    Why a spiral

    A golden spiral grows by φ every quarter turn. The vortex is drawn from that ratio, so it tightens at the same rate the breaths shorten.

    Why even

    Inhale and exhale always match. There's no holding and no forcing, only a steady in and out that gets shorter.

    Why one minute

    It's short enough to do between two other things, which makes it easy to come back to.

    How it works

    1. Press Begin64 seconds. Eyes can stay open.
    2. In as it opens, out as it closesFollow the spiral. Inhale and exhale are always the same length.
    3. Each breath is shorter13, 8, 5, 3, 2, 1, until there is nothing left to count. The softer start begins at 8.
