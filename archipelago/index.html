What it does

Archipelago is a free atlas of the Philippines. Pick a metric and the whole map recolors. Tap any region, or any row in the ranking, to see its number.

Each metric gets its own color scale, stretched from the lowest region to the highest, so the map always shows who's at the top and bottom on that measure. Brighter means more. For poverty and unemployment the scale turns warm, because more means worse.

The “regional average” is the plain average of the seventeen regions, each counted once. It isn't the national figure, which weights regions by size. The national number is listed under each source.

How it works

  1. Pick a readingPopulation, GDP per capita, poverty, internet at home or unemployment.
  2. Watch it recolorBrighter means more. Poverty and unemployment turn warm, because more means worse.
  3. Tap a regionOr any row in the ranking, to see its number and its rank of 17. Drag to pan, + and − to zoom.

The five readings

Population

How many people live in each region. Calabarzon, the National Capital Region and Central Luzon, the band around Manila, hold about 4 in 10 Filipinos.

PSA 2020 Census of Population and Housing
GDP per capita

Each region's economic output divided by its population, in current pesos. Metro Manila sits far above every other region.

PSA Regional Accounts, 2025
Poverty

The share of families living below the official poverty line. The highest rates are in Zamboanga, Bangsamoro, Bicol and Eastern Visayas.

PSA 2023 Official Poverty Statistics
Internet at home

The share of households with internet access at home. Roughly half the country is connected, with a wide gap between Metro Manila and Zamboanga.

PSA–DICT National ICT Household Survey, 2024
Unemployment

The share of the labor force without work. It runs highest in Calabarzon and Metro Manila.

PSA 2025 Annual Labor Force Survey (preliminary)
The map itself

Region outlines are from the 2023 boundaries of the Philippine Standard Geographic Code, simplified so the page loads fast. Some very small islands are left out.
