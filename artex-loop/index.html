The Loop That Doesn't Stop

How ARTEX keeps working until the goal is proved — the planner, the two graphs, and the anti-stall machinery — plus the 12 patterns extracted for our pipeline and the build order. Everything on this page is interactive: run the loop, break it, cut it off, and watch what it does.

1 · Run the loop

Three goals. One planner — the only thing allowed to create work. Workers claim one intent at a time on a lease. Press things. Try to make it stall.

STALL — goals unmet and zero work in flight. By ARTEX's rule this state is illegal: the planner is forced to emit new intents.

What to notice: a killed worker's intent isn't lost — its lease expires and the intent returns to the frontier. A cut run doesn't drop work either — a settlement round runs with tools disabled and lands everything already found. And the moment the frontier empties with goals still open, the hard floor fires.
