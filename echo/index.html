ECHO.

Your microphone is the sensor. One clap reveals the room.

Try Echo now →

Processed on your phone. Your recording is not uploaded or saved.

What it is

Echo is the sibling of Pulse: Pulse uses the camera to reveal a hidden biological signal, Echo uses the microphone to reveal a hidden spatial signal. Clap once and the sound coming back from the room becomes a waveform, an approximate tail reading, and a visual room signature — drawn from your actual capture.

Direct sound is the clap travelling straight from your hands to the phone. Reflected sound is the same clap bouncing off the walls, floor, ceiling and furniture, arriving a fraction later, over and over, each time a little quieter. Echo listens to that fading trail.

Under the Hood

  • 1. Clap. One sharp clap, near the phone. No speaker sweep, no playback.
  • 2. Reflections. The microphone captures the direct sound and the returns that follow it.
  • 3. Signature. The decay becomes a tail reading and a piece of artwork unique to the room.

The full story page — reflection timings, spectral texture, room comparison — follows once the core has been tested on a real phone. For now the demo does the core: capture, waveform, approximate tail, signature art, microphone off.
