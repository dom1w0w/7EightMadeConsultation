How it works

Direct sound is the clap travelling straight from your hands to the phone. Reflected sound is the same clap bouncing off walls, floor, ceiling and furniture, arriving a fraction later, over and over, each time a little quieter. Echo listens to that fading trail and draws it.

Processed on your phone. Your recording is not uploaded or saved — it lives only in this tab, and refreshing or closing the tab removes it. The microphone is off until you press Start listening, and it switches off again the moment the capture ends. Microphone audio is never played back through the speaker.
