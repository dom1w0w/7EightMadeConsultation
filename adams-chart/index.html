The Stream of Time

4004 BC – AD 1881

Every nation is a stream. It springs up, widens in power, splits, is swallowed by a larger river, or runs dry. The heavy pillars mark centuries; the small ticks, decades.

How to read it

  • Click any stream for its story.
  • Click a gold diamond for a great synchronism — a moment when the streams cross.
  • The vertical line follows your pointer: everything it touches was happening at once.

Dates before Christ follow Adams' Ussher chronology, as on the original chart.
