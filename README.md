## What we do

- **AI micro-consulting** — assistants trained on your SOPs, docs, and tone; automations across email, sheets, CRMs, and helpdesks; dashboards for KPIs and dealflow.
- **Fixed-scope pilots** — live in 24–72 hours. No lock-in. You own everything.
- **Private by default** — cloud-native on the stack you already run, or a fully local AI stack on hardware you own. Zero data leaves your network.

Built on the tools you already use: Salesforce · Google Workspace · Microsoft 365 · Notion · Airtable · HubSpot · Slack · Zapier / Make.
