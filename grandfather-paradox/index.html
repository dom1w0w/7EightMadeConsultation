Grandfather Paradox

Go back. Stop him. Watch yourself disappear.

Open the app → See how it works

What it does

Your family tree glows: you at the bottom, three generations above you, your grandfather at the top of the line that made you. Every branch had to happen exactly the way it happened. Then the app hands you a time machine.

You drop into 1952, the night your grandparents meet, and you get one choice: stop him. The moment you do, the tree above you starts erasing branch by branch — and your own portrait fades with it. Then the paradox lands: if you vanish, nobody went back, so he meets her, so you're born, so you go back.

The screen loops and flickers between both timelines until you pick an escape: a fixed timeline that protects itself, branching worlds that spare you, or a paradox that simply never resolves. It takes about ninety seconds, one hand on your phone, and it ends with a question.

How it works

  1. See your treeFour generations, lit up. The golden line is the one that leads to you.
  2. Go back to 1952One tap on the time machine, one choice: stop your grandfather.
  3. Watch it eraseBranch by branch, generation by generation — yours goes last.
  4. Pick your escapeFixed timeline, branching worlds, or the loop that never resolves.
